# Referências

1. **TADELIS, Steven.** Reputation and Feedback Systems in Online Platform Markets. _Annual Review of Economics_, v. 8, p. 321–340, 2016.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-economics-080315-015325

2. **JIAO, Ruohuang; PRZEPIORKA, Wojtek; BUSKENS, Vincent.** Reputation effects in peer-to-peer online markets: A meta-analysis. _Social Science Research_, v. 95, p. 102522, 2021.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssresearch.2020.102522

3. **LUCA, Michael; ZERVAS, Georgios.** Fake It Till You Make It: Reputation, Competition, and Yelp Review Fraud. _Management Science_, v. 62, n. 12, p. 3412–3427, 2016.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2015.2304

4. **OSBORNE, Martin J.** _An Introduction to Game Theory_. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2004.  
   ISBN: 978-0-19-512895-6

6. **NOSKO, Chris; TADELIS, Steven.** The Limits of Reputation in Platform Markets: An Empirical Analysis and Field Experiment. _Quantitative Marketing and Economics_, v. 24, art. 9, 2026.
